Important LTPOMS Design Principle
The Organization Unit hierarchy and Position reporting hierarchy are intentionally independent.
For example, Tube Plant may exist organizationally under Sub-Plant 2, while the Tube Plant Section Manager's actual reporting authority is determined separately by the Position Reporting structure.
This makes reorganizations, employee transfers, additional charge assignments and future LTPOMS modules possible without redesigning the core database.
