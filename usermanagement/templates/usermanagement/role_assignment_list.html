LTPOMS Role Assignment Model
Organizational authority should normally be assigned to a Position rather than directly to a User.
A direct User assignment should be used when the authority genuinely belongs to that specific account, such as controlled administrative or special-purpose access.
Position Assignment: authority follows the Position and is inherited by the Employee currently occupying that Position.
Scope: Organization Unit, Module, and descendant settings determine where an assignment applies.
Important: a Role Assignment without Scope records is currently treated as unrestricted by the LTPOMS authorization service.
